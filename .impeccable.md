## Design Context

### Users
Kiln engineers, quality analysts, reviewers, and auditors in a wood-processing plant. They use the tool at a workstation or on a tablet to inspect safety bounds, register lots, import moisture readings, and review explainable drying suggestions. The product is offline decision support and simulation; it never controls equipment.

### Brand Personality
Credible, calm, precise. The interface should make evidence and safety constraints easy to scan, with language that respects expert judgment.

### Aesthetic Direction
Industrial/utilitarian with warm kiln and wood accents: ink navy, parchment, copper, and measured status colors. Dense but breathable operational workspace, light-first, no gradients, neon, cyberpunk, decorative blobs, ecommerce dashboards, or excessive rounded cards.

### Design Principles
1. Put kiln boundaries, moisture evidence, and risk beside every decision.
2. Use clear hierarchy and compact tables for repeated operational work.
3. Preserve critical actions and readable evidence on mobile with touch-sized controls.
4. Show loading, empty, conflict, and permission states as part of the workflow.
5. Respect reduced motion and accessible labels; color never carries meaning alone.
